We'd love to hear from you, whether you've found a mistake, have a question about your results, or want to use the test in a class or project.

**Email:** [contact@strooptester.com](mailto:contact@strooptester.com)

We read everything and try to reply within a few working days.

## Helpful things to include

- **Corrections:** the page address and what you think is wrong. A link to a source helps a lot.
- **Questions about results:** which level you took and the numbers you got. Please don't send us anything you'd rather keep private, like health details.
- **Teaching or research use:** a line about what you have in mind. The test is free to use for teaching demonstrations. For research, bear in mind it hasn't been validated as a research instrument.

## What we can't help with

We can't give medical advice or interpret results for diagnosis. If you're worried about your attention, memory or thinking, please talk to your doctor or another qualified health professional.
