The short version: **your test answers and scores never leave your device.** We only use analytics cookies if you say yes.

## Your test results

The Stroop test runs entirely in your browser. Your responses, response times and scores are calculated on your device and are **not sent to us or anyone else**.

So you can see your history, the test saves a short summary of your last 30 results (date, level, accuracy, typical response and interference) in your browser's local storage. That data stays on your device. You can delete it at any time by clearing this site's data in your browser settings.

If you use **Share result**, the link contains only the summary numbers shown on the results screen, encoded in the address. Anyone you send the link to can see those numbers. Nothing is stored on our side.

If you use **Download raw data**, the file is created in your browser and saved to your device.

## Analytics

With your permission, we use Google Analytics 4 to understand how many people visit, which pages they read and roughly where they come from. This helps us decide what to write and improve. Google Analytics sets cookies and processes data such as your approximate location, device type and the pages you view.

- Analytics **only loads after you click "Allow analytics"** in the cookie banner.
- If you click "No thanks", or ignore the banner, no analytics scripts or cookies are loaded.
- You can change your mind at any time with the **Cookie settings** link at the bottom of every page.
- We ask Google to anonymise IP addresses, and we don't use advertising features.

Google's own privacy policy is at [policies.google.com/privacy](https://policies.google.com/privacy).

We store your cookie choice in your browser's local storage so we don't ask you on every page.

## Hosting

The site is a set of static files. Our hosting and network providers (such as GitHub and Cloudflare) process standard technical data, like your IP address, to deliver pages and protect against abuse. They don't receive your test results.

## Email

If you email us, we use your message and address only to reply, and we delete correspondence when it's no longer needed.

## Children

The site is meant for a general audience and doesn't knowingly collect personal information from children.

## Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example under the UK GDPR or EU GDPR), you may have rights to access, correct or delete personal data about you, and to object to processing. Since we don't hold your test data, most requests will be about analytics, which you can switch off at any time. For anything else, [contact us](/contact/).

## Changes

If we change this policy, we'll update the date at the top of the page.
